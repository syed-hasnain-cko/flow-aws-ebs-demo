3DS · Non-hosted (browser channel)

With a non-hosted session the merchant's own page runs the authentication: it asks Checkout.com for a session, gathers the browser's 3DS Method fingerprint and device data, and renders the issuer's challenge itself. This tab is a working reference of those six steps with a live request and response for each. The challenge is shown in the presentation theme you pick, so you can compare how a modal, a bottom sheet or a full-screen takeover feels. Browser channel only — the app channel needs Checkout.com's iOS / Android 3DS SDKs.

The content of the challenge (password page, OTP, biometric prompt) is rendered by the issuer's ACS inside an iframe and cannot be restyled by the merchant. What the merchant controls — and what these themes change — is the container: layout, colours, header, loader, window size (challengeWindowSize) and how a cancelled challenge is handled.
