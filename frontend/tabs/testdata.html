🧪 Test Data Generator

Create realistic sandbox transactions in an account, with no one at the keyboard — card payments (customer-initiated and merchant-initiated), payouts to cards and banks, declines and disputes. Nobody can complete a 3D Secure challenge here, so customer payments are sent without 3DS and merchant-initiated payments are backend-only. Every transaction is tagged with a batch reference so you can find it again. Sandbox only — a live key is refused.

Amounts & currencies what the payments look like
Payment currencies
Disputes always use GBP (the sandbox only simulates them in GBP). Payouts use EUR — Pay to bank is EUR-only, and Pay to card was accepted in EUR on the demo account.
Cards which brands to use
⚠️ Only tick brands that are enabled for your sandbox account (and its processing channel) — ask your Checkout.com contact to enable a brand first. If the API refuses a ticked brand, the generator skips that brand and carries on with the ones that work. A brand that is enabled but whose payments are declined is not skipped: those show up as “unexpected” in the results.
Declined payments which decline codes can appear
Each declined payment gets a code picked at random from the ticked ones, so a merchant sees a realistic mix in their reports. verified = tried on the demo account; documented = from the Checkout.com test-cards page. Only codes whose card brand is ticked in “Cards” are used.
Disputes which dispute outcomes to simulate
Sandbox disputes are triggered by a payment with a special amount, card 01/2099 expiry and currency GBP. Checkout.com raises each dispute a few minutes after the payment (in testing between about 6 and 10), so they appear in the dashboard shortly after generation — use “Check disputes now” on the results.
Follow-up actions what happens to the one-off customer payments afterwards
Out of the 0 approved one-off payments, how many should end up in each state? The rest are simply captured right away.
