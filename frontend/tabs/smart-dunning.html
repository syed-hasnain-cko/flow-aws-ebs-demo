Smart Dunning

Recover failed merchant-initiated payments (recurring / unscheduled). When an MIT is soft-declined, Checkout.com schedules retries at the best times and sends webhooks at every step. Pick a scenario, tune the retry schedule, run it and watch the whole life of the payment — every attempt, every webhook and the final state. Needs a direct Checkout.com integration. In the sandbox the schedule is compressed (retries about 30 seconds apart) regardless of the values below. The tab listens for webhooks and updates by itself; Refresh reloads on demand.
