3. Account Updater & Real-Time Account Updater

    
⚠️
Requires enablement, and CKO publishes no sandbox test cards. Ask your account manager to enable the Standalone Account Updater / RTAU (Mastercard Store & Update, Update & Retry; Visa Account Updater). Without it you will see 401/403/422 — the UI shows exactly what came back. Use any card numbers your team has been given.


    
A · Standalone Account Updater — POST /account-updater/cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)

    
Uses the card source above: a saved instrument sends source_options.instrument.id; a raw card sends source_options.card. Amex updates run in batch — request at least 24h before use.

    
Request preview

    

    


    
B · Real-Time Account Updater — triggers inside POST /payments

    
RTAU fires automatically on eligible payments: a MIT or card-on-file CIT, using a stored instrument or full card details (not network tokens), amount > 0. The outcome is in the payment's source.account_update_status, and the card_updated webhook carries the original and replacement card.
